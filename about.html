About the agency

Built by people who were once on the other side of the table.

I² Models represents editorial, commercial, runway, and fitness talent. The agency is built around direct representation, clear terms, and a roster small enough that every model is known by name.

Our approach

We take on fewer models than most agencies our size so every person on the roster gets direct attention from an agent, not a rotating account team.

Who we work with

We work with brands, publications, casting directors, and productions that need reliable talent for editorial, commercial, runway, and campaign work.

What we ask in return

Reliability, professionalism on set, and honesty with us about your availability and goals. We plan careers, not one-off gigs.

Where we're headed

We are growing our fitness and commercial categories and reviewing applications for both.
