Privacy

We collect only what is needed to run the agency and this site.

What we receive

  • Contact details and messages sent through the contact or application forms, or by email.
  • Application information such as name, date of birth, location, measurements, category, portfolio links, and photographs you attach.
  • Booking details supplied by clients and by represented talent.
  • Technical data created by ordinary use of the site, such as browser type and pages requested.

Information is used to review applications, maintain the roster, arrange bookings, administer programmes, respond to enquiries, and keep the site secure. We do not sell personal information.

Forms on this site open a message in your own email application addressed to the agency inbox. Once sent, that message is handled as ordinary business correspondence.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you by writing to the contact address below. Representation

Where a model is represented, the signed agreement is the contract. This page does not replace it. If there is a difference between this page and a signed agreement, the signed agreement governs that point.

Represented talent are independent contractors, not employees of I² Models. Nothing on this site creates an employment relationship, a guarantee of bookings, or a guarantee of income. Work depends on client demand, the model’s availability and suitability, and a confirmed booking.

Commission, payment timing, exclusivity, notice, and the end of representation are as set out in the signed agreement. Public statements on this site about how the agency works are descriptive and are not additional promises.

Bookings

A booking is confirmed only in writing from the agency — by email or other written confirmation issued by us. Enquiries, holds, and verbal discussions are not confirmations.

The confirmation sets the date, fee, usage, and any special conditions that have been agreed. Usage of images or footage is limited to what that confirmation (and any related release) records. Wider or later use requires a further written agreement and, where applicable, a further fee.

Clients are responsible for a safe, lawful set and for the conduct of their own crew and contractors. The agency introduces talent and administers the booking. It does not supervise the production and is not the employer of crew or of the model on set.

Fees are due as stated on the confirmation or invoice. Where the agency collects a client fee on behalf of talent, sums due to the model are remitted after cleared receipt, less commission and any amounts the signed agreement permits. The agency is not required to advance fees that a client has failed to pay. Cancellation, overtime, weather, and unused options follow the written confirmation and, if none is stated there, the agency’s standard booking terms supplied with the confirmation.

The agency may decline or withdraw a booking that cannot be performed as agreed, or that would require the agency or the model to act unlawfully or outside the confirmed terms.

Represented models grant the agency a limited licence to use their name, approved measurements, and approved images on this website and in ordinary agency materials for the purpose of seeking and administering work, for the period of representation and for a reasonable time afterwards while pages are updated.

That licence is not a transfer of copyright. It does not allow the agency to sell a model’s image as stock, and it does not replace a client usage grant for a specific job.

When representation ends, we will remove roster images from this site within a reasonable time after written request. We are not required to recall press, client work, or archived pages already published in the ordinary course of business.

If you believe an image appears here without the right to display it, write to the contact address with a precise link and a short statement of your claim. We will review complete notices and remove or restore material as the facts require.

Minors

Applicants who are under eighteen must have a parent or legal guardian complete and submit the application and any later agreement. Guardians are responsible for work permits, schooling constraints, and any other legal condition that applies to the young person’s work. Bookings involving minors proceed only on terms the guardian has accepted in writing.

Concerns can be sent to the contact address on this page. We will record the report and review it. Reporting a concern does not, by itself, create a finding, a payment obligation, or a right to a particular outcome. Criminal matters should also be raised with the appropriate authorities.

We may decline to work with a client or to continue a booking where we reasonably believe the arrangement cannot be carried out safely or as confirmed.

Programme fees, unless the enrolment terms say otherwise, are not refundable once a place is confirmed, except where the agency cancels the intake and cannot offer a reasonable alternative date.
