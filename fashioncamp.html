Fashion Camp

Train like the roster.

Fashion Camp is a practical training program for new and developing talent. Sessions cover runway, posing, camera work, portfolio direction, and how to carry yourself on set.

Who it's for

Open to new faces and working models who want structured time with agents and working talent. Applicants should be ready to train, take direction, and treat the room like a booking.

What's covered

Runway walk, posing for stills and movement, camera presence, portfolio building, and the standards expected on a professional set.

Schedule

Intakes run in focused sessions. Dates, duration, and location are confirmed with each group so the room stays small and the work stays specific.

Cost

Tuition and what is included are sent with enrollment details. Reach out for the next intake and we will send the current package.
